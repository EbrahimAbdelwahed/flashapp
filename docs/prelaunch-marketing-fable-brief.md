# Flash Up — Pre-launch Marketing Brief for Fable

## Purpose

Turn the nearly finished application into a small, measurable pre-launch
campaign that validates demand before paying for the Apple Developer Program.

Use [`../flash-up-marketing-brief.md`](../flash-up-marketing-brief.md) as the
source of truth for audience, positioning, promises, and copy constraints. Do
not redefine the product or invent unimplemented features.

## Current situation

- The iPhone/iPad application is close to complete.
- CloudKit configuration and App Store publication require the annual Apple
  Developer membership.
- The launch price is €1.99.
- The immediate goal is to prove enough purchase intent to justify that initial
  cost without wasting a viral moment before the app is available.

## Approved strategy

1. Finish the application and prepare a deterministic demo state containing no
   personal data.
2. Publish a simple waitlist landing page before releasing marketing videos.
3. Produce an initial batch of 10–15 vertical videos using the real application
   interface and real product flows.
4. Use a small number of reusable formats with different hooks rather than
   generating unrelated ads.
5. Give every video the same primary call to action: join the waitlist to
   receive the App Store link at launch.
6. Review every video before publication.
7. Schedule one or two videos per day on TikTok and Instagram Reels.
8. Track waitlist sign-ups per creative; views alone are not validation.
9. Build a larger generation and publishing pipeline only after a format proves
   that it converts.

## Video constraints

- Use screen recordings and frames from the real app; do not synthesize or
  distort the interface with video models.
- Begin with the student’s problem, not “I built an app.”
- Demonstrate the ChatGPT → CSV → import → study journey.
- State the one-time €1.99 price only when the creative needs the commercial
  contrast.
- Do not attack Anki; attack subscription fatigue and unnecessary complexity.
- Do not claim that the app is already downloadable.
- Keep the waitlist CTA explicit and visible.
- Optimize for 9:16 short-form video.

## Initial creative families

Create variations across three families:

1. **Subscription problem:** studying should not require another monthly fee.
2. **Import friction:** the student already has cards from ChatGPT and wants to
   begin reviewing them immediately.
3. **Study outcome:** spaced repetition and synchronization are handled so the
   student can focus on reviewing.

The first batch should test different hooks and demonstrations while preserving
the same product truth and CTA.

## Validation

The campaign must measure:

- landing-page visits;
- waitlist conversion rate;
- qualified sign-ups from university students using Apple devices;
- sign-ups attributed to each hook and video format;
- explicit comfort with the €1.99 launch price.

Use approximately 300 qualified waitlist sign-ups and a visit-to-sign-up rate
above 10% as an initial validation threshold, not as a guarantee of sales.

## What Fable should produce next

1. A concise landing-page narrative and waitlist CTA.
2. Three repeatable video format definitions.
3. 10–15 complete video briefs containing:
   - hook;
   - problem;
   - required app flow or screen recording;
   - overlay copy;
   - optional voiceover;
   - final CTA;
   - TikTok caption;
   - Instagram caption.
4. A publishing calendar for one or two posts per day.
5. A measurement plan that identifies which creative generated each sign-up.

Keep the plan executable by one person. Do not introduce paid tool stacks,
avatars, automated publishers, or a custom rendering pipeline unless a proven
creative format later creates enough volume to justify them.
